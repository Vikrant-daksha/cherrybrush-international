The effect in those circles is usually described as **iridescent / pearlescent glass texture** or **gemstone-like refraction**.

For your specific examples, a few terms fit:

- **Iridescent** — shifting pink/peach tones with light-like color variation.
- **Pearlescent** — soft, milky glow with subtle pink/white reflections.
- **Opalescent** — translucent, cloudy, luminous color shifts; probably closest to your examples.
- **Prismatic** — stronger rainbow/crystal-like light dispersion.
- **Caustic / light refraction** — those bright curved patches that look like light passing through glass or a crystal.
- **Faceted / gemstone texture** — angular highlights that make the surface look like a diamond.
- **Glassmorphism** — if you're referring to the overall UI treatment rather than the texture itself.
